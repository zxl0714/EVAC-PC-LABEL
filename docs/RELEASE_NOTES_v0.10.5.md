# EVAC PC LABEL v0.10.5

## English

- Play the World and Japanese arcade versions of DoDonPachi through CapsLock4284’s DoDonPachi Recomp. In the launcher, select your ZIP or 7z ROM set. With a merged set, you can switch regions in the game menu. [Setup guide](GAME_DATA.en.md#dodonpachi) · [#32](https://github.com/zxl0714/EVAC-PC-LABEL/issues/32)
- Fixed a regression in Ibara that delayed the picture during gameplay. [#34](https://github.com/zxl0714/EVAC-PC-LABEL/issues/34)
- Fixed an issue under Proton that prevented Mushihimesama Futari from recognizing the original Black Label and Ver.1.01 DLC packages.
- Improved PS4 game compatibility under Proton. Ketsui can now reach gameplay on Vulkan devices with a smaller sparse address space.
- PS2 games can now use a software Vulkan driver such as Mesa llvmpipe when a hardware GPU is unavailable.

## 简体中文

- 接入 CapsLock4284 的《怒首领蜂》DoDonPachi Recomp，支持街机国际版和日版。在启动器里选择 ZIP 或 7z 格式的 ROM 即可。使用合并包时，可以在游戏菜单里切换地区。[数据放置说明](GAME_DATA.md#dodonpachi) · [#32](https://github.com/zxl0714/EVAC-PC-LABEL/issues/32)
- 修复《铸蔷薇》关内画面响应变慢的问题。[#34](https://github.com/zxl0714/EVAC-PC-LABEL/issues/34)
- 修复 Proton 下《虫姬二》无法识别 Black Label 和 Ver.1.01 原始 DLC 包的问题。
- 改善 PS4 游戏在 Proton 下的兼容性，修复《绊地狱》在部分 Vulkan 设备上停在加载画面的问题。
- PS2 游戏现在可以在没有硬件 GPU 的环境下使用 Mesa llvmpipe 等 Vulkan 软件驱动运行。
